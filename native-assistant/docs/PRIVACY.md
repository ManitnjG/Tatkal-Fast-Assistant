# Privacy notice

Tatkal Fast Assistant stores passenger names, ages, gender, nationality, preferences, journey dates/stations, contact mobile numbers and user-entered booking references locally in encrypted form. This supports reusable booking preparation and personal history. The app does not collect IRCTC passwords, Aadhaar numbers, CAPTCHA, OTP, card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN, cookies or payment tokens.

No developer server receives data. No analytics, advertising or crash reporting is configured. Opening IRCTC shares normal browser traffic with IRCTC and its payment providers under their policies. Copy/share transfers only the values you choose through Android facilities.

You can delete reusable passenger and journey profiles in the app. Historic attempt snapshots retain their original information for recovery; deleting a profile does not erase that history. To erase all records, use Android Settings > Apps > Tatkal Fast Assistant > Storage > Clear data or uninstall. First resolve any pending booking/payment using official history. No cloud backup is provided; clearing data or losing your device loses records. Device-level security and clipboard behavior remain outside this app's control.

App lock is enabled by default. Notifications are optional, generic and approximate. The app is an independent assistant, not an official IRCTC product.


Optional autofill: when selected in Android settings, the app receives form structures from Android. It checks the browser identity, web origin and field descriptors to offer a prepared value. It does not read or save existing field text, credentials or tokens. Unsupported apps and pages receive no suggestions. A chosen prepared field is sent to the requesting browser only after device authentication and explicit confirmation. Changing the system autofill provider affects the current provider, including password-manager autofill. You can switch back in Android settings.

Optional backup: encrypted profiles and booking records are written only to a user-chosen document location. That provider may be cloud-backed. Passphrases are not retained, transmitted or recoverable. Explicitly pasted confirmation text is processed locally to extract a labelled PNR and is not stored with the booking. Setup checks use local Android settings and installed Chrome certificate information.
